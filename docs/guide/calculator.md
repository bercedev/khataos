# Calculator

**What you'll do:** work out a figure in the calculator panel, add or take out tax, and put the answer into the field you were on.

## Open and close it

- Press `Ctrl+N` on any screen. You don't need a company open.
- In an amount, quantity, rate or value field, `Alt+C` also opens it, starting with the field's value.
- Press `Ctrl+N` again or `Esc` to close it. Focus goes back to your field.

The panel docks beside the screen (on the left in Arabic), so you can still see what you're working on. `Tab` goes back to the screen and leaves the panel open; `Ctrl+N` returns to it.

## Calculate

1. Type the sum, for example `1000 + 18%` or `(2500 * 3) - 450`.
2. Press `Enter`. The answer stays selected, so typing `+`, `-`, `*` or `/` carries on from it.

| You can use | Example |
|---|---|
| `+ - * /` and brackets | `(1200 + 800) / 4` |
| Add or take off a percent | `1000 + 18%` is 1180; `1180 - 10%` is 1062 |
| A percent of a number | `500 * 18%` is 90 |
| Digit grouping | `1,00,000` or `100,000` |
| Arabic-Indic digits | `١٢٣` |

The arithmetic is exact. The answer is rounded to your currency's smallest unit (2 decimals for most currencies, 3 for currencies such as KWD, 0 for JPY), and the panel shows the value before rounding when it differs.

There are no memory keys (M+, MR).

## Add or take out tax

1. Press `Alt+T`. It cycles through **Add {tax}**, **Take out {tax}** and off.
2. Press `Alt+R` (or `Alt+Shift+R`) to change the rate.
3. The panel shows the taxable value, the tax (for India, split into CGST and SGST) and the total.

The rates come from your company's country, for example 5, 12, 18, 28 and 40% GST in India, 5% VAT in the UAE, 15% in Saudi Arabia, 9% GST in Singapore, and 20 and 5% VAT in the UK. US companies have no preset rates.

## Use the answer

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Enter` | Calculate, and add the line to the history |
| `Ctrl+Enter` | Put the answer in the field you came from, and close the panel (or copy it, if that field doesn't take numbers) |
| `Shift+Enter` | Copy the answer |
| `↑` `↓` | Bring back an earlier line |
| `Alt+D` | Clear the history |

The history keeps the last 50 lines for each company and user, on this computer only. It is never saved in the company file.

## On a phone

The calculator opens as a sheet at the bottom with its own keypad. Tap the calculator button at the top of the screen.
