KhataOS/ThemesEvery pair meets WCAG 2.2 AA

Seven themes. Light and dark.

Each theme suits a different kind of user, and each comes in light and dark. Light, dark or Auto, the density and the text size are separate choices that work with every theme. Your choice is kept for you on your device, never in the company's books.

  • Ledger
  • Chartered
  • Executive
  • Terminal
  • Fresh
  • Comfort
  • Regional

Change theme with Ctrl+K → Theme

  1. Press Ctrl+K and choose Theme.
  2. Move with ↑ ↓. The screen changes as you go.
  3. Enter keeps the theme. Esc puts the old one back.

Printed and PDF invoices aren't themed: they keep their own invoice design.

01/Ledger

Ledger Default

For everyone

Calm and trustworthy. Ledger indigo for the top bar and titles, marigold for the spine, the cursor edge and the key letters, and warm paper behind white cards. It's the theme KhataOS starts with, in Auto mode.

Mode

02/Chartered

Chartered

For chartered accountants and auditors

Like print. Paper white, navy ink, red ink for accents and negative figures, serif report titles, and square corners with hairline rules instead of shadows, so a Trial Balance on screen reads like a signed statement.

Mode

03/Executive

Executive

For owners, CxOs and directors

Refined graphite with a quiet champagne brass accent, and larger key figures on the Dashboard and in report titles. Less chrome, numbers first, and easy to read on a boardroom screen.

Mode

04/Terminal

Terminal

For finance and tech enthusiasts

Dense, with the data first. Monospace figures line up digit for digit, corners are square, dark mode is near black with high contrast ink, and up is blue while down is orange. It pairs well with Compact density.

Mode

05/Fresh

Fresh

For young people and first businesses

Vibrant violet with a coral accent and softer, rounder corners, yet every colour pair still meets AA and nothing glows. Friendly for a first business or a side hustle, and professional enough to show a client.

Mode

06/Comfort

Comfort

For older users, low vision and long days

Accessibility first. Text, rows and click targets are 20% larger, on top of your own text size. Body and secondary text meet AAA contrast (7:1), field borders are solid, the focus ring is 3 px wide, and the cursor row is a pale yellow that is easy to follow.

Mode

07/Regional

Regional

For companies in India, the Gulf, the UK, and East Asia with Singapore

Four palettes drawn from local materials rather than flags. India: indigo and turmeric on sandal cream. Gulf: deep emerald and sand with muted gold. United Kingdom: heritage navy and brick red on stone. East Asia and Singapore: deep teal with lacquer red, and blue and orange for profit and loss, because red means up on East Asian markets. KhataOS picks the palette from the company's country, or you choose one.

Mode
