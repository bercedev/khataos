# TallyPrime feature gaps: accounting, vouchers, masters and reports

> **Purpose.** This file lists what TallyPrime does in accounting, vouchers, masters, reports and year-end work, and checks each item against KhataOS. It tells us what to build next for accountants who switch from Tally.
>
> **Clean room.** Every description below is in our own words and based on Tally's public help pages, product pages and tutorials. No Tally binary was run or inspected, nobody logged in anywhere, and no Tally screen layout, field wording or image was copied. Where a common accounting term matches Tally's (for example "cost centre" or "post-dated"), it is used because it is the ordinary term.
>
> **Researched:** 2026-10-21, against TallyPrime 7.x public help. Several Tally help pages are only published for Tally.ERP 9 or for older TallyPrime releases. Those URLs are marked *(ERP 9 docs)*, and the feature should be checked in a current TallyPrime before it is treated as a spec.
>
> **Companion files:** `docs/research/tally-shortcuts.md` (key map), `docs/research/tally-pain-points.md`.
>
> **Out of scope here:** inventory and orders (stock items, godowns, delivery and receipt notes, negative stock), payroll, GST, TDS, VAT and e-invoicing. Those have their own research files.

## How to read the tables

- **Status:**
  - **have** means KhataOS does the job a Tally user expects.
  - **partial** means part of it is there, or it exists without the Tally key or view.
  - **missing** means there is nothing yet. A key bound in `crates/khata-keymap/src/tally.rs` with no handler still counts as missing.
- **Effort:**
  - **S** is a few days.
  - **M** is one to two weeks.
  - **L** is three weeks or more, or it changes the data model.
- **Priority:**
  - **P1** means a user switching from Tally will miss it on day one, or migrated data loses meaning without it.
  - **P2** is weekly or monthly work.
  - **P3** is advanced or niche.
- **File** paths are relative to the repo root. In them, "FE" is `apps/desktop/src/screens/`, "BE" is `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/` and "core" is `crates/khata-core/src/`.

## Notes from the codebase check

- **Bound but unhandled keys.** Many Tally keys are already in `crates/khata-keymap/src/tally.rs` with no screen handling them. These include Ctrl+B, Ctrl+J, Ctrl+F (in reports), Ctrl+L, Alt+C, Alt+N, Alt+Q, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+L (in vouchers), Alt+2, Alt+I, Ctrl+R, Alt+R, F4 (in reports), Shift+Enter and Ctrl+Alt+U. A switcher presses them and nothing happens. A short "not available yet" toast like the one the shell gives for `NOT_YET` actions would be kinder until each is built. *Updated 2026-10-21:* the shell's `NOT_BUILT` set now gives that message for most of them; Ctrl+F in reports (Filter) and Alt+C/Alt+D in reports outside Tax Rates still do nothing.
- **The importer drops data silently.** `khata-import-tally` drops bill allocations, cost centre allocations, bank and instrument allocations, optional vouchers and custom voucher type identity. It still balances, but outstandings and ageing come out empty after migrating from Tally. That is why M5, M9 and M13 are P1.
- **The trail design makes year-end simpler.** Because balances are worked out from one continuous trail, "close the year" and "carry forward" need no special step (Y1). The remaining year-end gaps are presentation (Y4), locking (Y5) and archiving (Y3).
