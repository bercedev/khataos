# Mobile

**What you'll do:** use KhataOS on an Android phone or an iPhone, and see what works differently there.

There are no app-store releases yet. To try KhataOS on a phone, build it from source: see [Install](install.md#android-and-ios).

KhataOS on a phone is the same app as on the desktop, with the same books and keys. It needs Android 7.0 or newer, or iOS 15 or newer.

## Getting around with touch

- **Tap** any button on the bars: it does the same as its key.
- The **F1–F12** button opens a drawer with the function keys (`F8` for Sales and so on).
- The **Back** button, or Android's back gesture, is `Esc`.
- Lists open as sheets from the bottom of the screen. Forms are one column.
- Amount fields bring up the number keypad.
- The calculator button at the top opens the calculator with its own keypad.
- With a hardware keyboard attached, every shortcut works as on a computer, and key hints appear once you press a key.

## Your books on the phone

Company files are kept in the app's private storage. To work on the same books as your office or accountant, connect to them through the relay or the office network, or open an accountant file (`.khatapkg`). See [Sharing](sharing.md). You can import Tally XML files from the phone too (up to 200 MB each).

## What doesn't work on a phone yet

- **Printing**, and saving a report as PDF through printing. Use `Ctrl+E` (CSV) on a report, or the PDF in a saved invoice's Export menu (`Ctrl+E`), or print from a computer.
- **Acting as the office server.** A phone can connect to an office server, but can't be one.
- **Quitting** on iOS (close the app the usual way).
- There is no way to delete a company from inside the app yet.
- A share sheet, native printing and a tablet layout are planned.

Developers: see [Android and iOS](../mobile.md).
