# Tally import readiness review

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Scope:** review only. The importer was not changed; two desktop items found here were fixed alongside the backup work (the import command is now async, and it asks and backs up before importing into a company with books). This looks at what `khata-import-tally` and the desktop import path do with a real TallyPrime export, before the maintainer imports real company data.
- **Read:** `crates/khata-import-tally/src/*` (lib, parse, apply, text, request, http), its tests and fixtures, `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/export.rs` (`import_tally`), `commands.rs` (`import_tally`), `phone_files.rs` (`local_input`, `read_bytes`), `apps/desktop/src/screens/company.ts` (`importFromTally`), `reports.ts` (`ImportReportScreen`), and `khata-core` (`book.rs`, `voucher.rs`, `chart.rs`, `inventory.rs`).
- **How it was checked:** reading the code, running `cargo test -p khata-import-tally` (38 tests pass), and a throwaway program outside the repo. That program fed the importer hand-made Tally-shaped XML for each edge case, and timed synthetic Day Books of 100,000 and 300,000 vouchers. Behaviour marked "verified" was seen in that program. Behaviour marked "not verified" is read from the code, or is an assumption about what Tally writes.
- **Caveat:** every fixture in `crates/khata-import-tally/tests/fixtures` is hand-written ("Hand-written sample in the shape of a TallyPrime … file"). No real TallyPrime export has been run through the importer, so the exact tag layout Tally writes is itself unverified.

## Summary: the five findings most likely to bite

1. **Repeated voucher numbers drop real vouchers.** KhataOS needs a number to be unique per base kind (Sales, Payment, …) and numbering period. Tally numbers each custom voucher type on its own ("Cash Sales 1", "Credit Sales 1"), and often restarts every year. Every clash after the first is skipped. The skip is reported, but the books then don't match Tally. Verified.
2. **Contra vouchers using a Bank OD or Bank OCC (cash credit) ledger are skipped.** KhataOS marks Bank OD A/c and Bank OCC A/c as not cash or bank (`chart.rs` `DEFAULT_GROUPS`), so `check_voucher` rejects the Contra. Tally allows these, and traders with a CC limit use them daily. Verified.
3. **Bills, cost centres, bank dates, GST registration data and supplier references are dropped without a report line.** Totals stay right. But outstandings and ageing are empty, cost centre reports are empty, and bank reconciliation must be redone. GSTINs and states are missing, and tax ledgers are not tagged, so KhataOS GST reports can't see imported tax lines. The guide lists most of this, but the result screen says nothing. Verified for bills, cost centres, GSTIN and reference.
4. **A partial Day Book gives silently wrong balances.** Ledger opening balances in a Tally masters export are as at the books beginning date. If the Day Book covers only recent years, every closing balance is wrong, yet the trial balance still tallies. Nothing warns about it. Read from the code. Tally's opening semantics are not verified in a live Tally here.
5. **Large charts of accounts are slow, and the window freezes.** Every voucher line looks its ledger up with a linear scan (`Book::ledger_by_name`, called first by `resolve_ledger`). The desktop command is a plain synchronous Tauri command, so the import runs on the main thread. Measured: 100,000 vouchers took 10.6 s with 3,000 ledgers and 54 s with 10,000 ledgers. Nothing is lost, but a user may kill a frozen app. Nothing is saved until the end, so killing it leaves the company unchanged.

What works well, verified in tests or the probe:

- UTF-16 LE and BE, with or without BOM, and UTF-8 with or without BOM (`text.rs` `decode`).
- `&#4;` and other control character references are stripped before parsing (`text.rs` `sanitize`). `&#13;&#10;` survives into narrations as CR LF.
- `&amp;` and the other predefined entities work. Unknown entities are dropped instead of failing (`parse.rs` `predefined`). CDATA is read.
- Amounts use `rust_decimal`, never floats. These parse: Indian grouping (`1,00,000.00`), currency symbols, Dr/Cr prefixes and suffixes, parentheses, and the forex form `… = -₹ 830.00`. Quantities like `10.000 Nos` and `10 Nos = 1 Box` parse too.
- Sign follows the sign of `AMOUNT`, not `ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE`. That is right for Tally XML, including negative value lines.
- Masters and Day Book can come in any order and in any file, because `import_all` parses everything first and applies masters before vouchers. Groups, stock groups and godowns are created in dependency order.
- A file that is not Tally XML, is truncated, or carries Tally `STATUS` 0 aborts before the book is touched.

## Ranked findings

Ranked by how likely each is to bite on real data, then by severity. "Effect" uses three values:

- **fails loudly:** an error, and nothing is imported;
- **skipped and reported:** the object is left out, with a line on the result screen;
- **silently wrong:** imported wrong or incomplete, with no line on the result screen.

Checked and found fine on the points the brief asked about:

- **Optional vouchers** are skipped. Tally keeps them out of the books too.
- **Memorandum and Reversing Journal** vouchers are skipped. They don't affect Tally's books either.
- **Invoice view and voucher view** both work: `read_voucher` uses whichever of `ALLLEDGERENTRIES.LIST`, `LEDGERENTRIES.LIST` plus inventory allocations, or both, balances.
- **Unbalanced vouchers** are rejected by `Voucher::validate` and reported.
- **Voucher dates** outside the financial year are accepted. There is no date check.
- **Long narrations** have no length limit.
- **Aliases** from `LANGUAGENAME.LIST` / `NAME.LIST` are kept. They never shadow a real ledger name.
- **Ledgers that differ only in case** can't occur in Tally, and lookups ignore ASCII case.
- **Unknown ledgers** in vouchers are reported.

## Before importing real data

1. **Start fresh.** Create a new company in KhataOS. Set the country to India and the financial year to start in April.
2. **Set numbering first.** If Tally restarts numbering each year, set every voucher type's numbering to restart yearly before the import (Settings, voucher numbering). Verified: this stops next-year duplicates. It does not help two custom types of the same base type that share numbers in one year.
3. **Write down Tally's figures** for the last date: trial balance totals, the opening difference, the closing balances of cash, every bank and CC account and the largest parties, and voucher counts by type (Tally's Statistics report).
4. **Export All Masters**, including voucher types, as XML.
5. **Export the Day Book from the books beginning date** to the last date, not just recent years. Unicode (UTF-16) or UTF-8 both work. Avoid the ASCII option if any name uses Indian scripts.
6. **Import both files in one go.** Select both in the file dialog. Use the desktop app, not a phone: phones refuse files over 200 MB.
7. **Expect a pause.** On a large file the window may look frozen for a minute or more. Don't force quit. If you do, nothing is saved.
8. **Read the whole skip list,** grouped by reason. Look for Contra vouchers on CC or OD accounts, duplicate numbers, unknown voucher types and custom primary groups. These change balances.
9. **Compare with Tally:** the trial balance totals, the "Difference in opening balances" row (the trial balance and balance sheet show it when it isn't zero), and the cash, bank, CC and party balances you wrote down.
10. **Know what is missing even when nothing is skipped:** bills (outstandings and ageing start empty), cost centres, GSTINs and states, GST tagging on CGST, SGST and IGST ledgers, bank reconciliation dates, supplier bill numbers and foreign amounts. Before running any GST report, set the tax component on each duty ledger.
11. **Don't import the same files twice** into one company. To retry, delete the test company and start again. Keep Tally's own data folder backed up and untouched.

## Trivial fixes I'd suggest (not applied)

1. In `apply.rs` `resolve_ledger`, look in `self.ledgers` first and fall back to `Book::ledger_by_name`. The map already holds every imported and reused ledger name, so the result is the same and the linear scan goes away.
2. ~~Make the desktop `import_tally` command `async`, or run the work in `spawn_blocking`, so the window stays responsive.~~ Done with the backup work.
3. In `parse.rs` `read_voucher`, skip vouchers with `ACTION="Delete"` or `ISDELETED` Yes, and report `ISPOSTDATED` Yes, each with a new `SkipReason`.
4. In `parse.rs` `read_stock_item`, check for a compound unit before parsing `OPENINGBALANCE`, so the reason is "compound unit", not "not a number".
5. In `apply.rs` `build_voucher`, post cancelled vouchers with `cancelled: true` instead of skipping them.
6. In `apply.rs` `remember_group`, key custom group names by lower case instead of `normalize`.
7. Append `REFERENCE` (and `REFERENCEDATE`) to the narration, for example "Ref: INV-0912 dt 03-04-2024".
8. Map `BANKERSDATE` in `BANKALLOCATIONS.LIST` to `Entry.bank_date` when the ledger is cash or bank.
9. Add to `ImportReport` and `ImportSummary`: the first and last voucher dates, `Book::opening_difference()`, trial balance totals, and counts of dropped bill, cost centre and bank allocations. Show them on `ImportReportScreen`.
10. Change the `DuplicateLedger` reason to say the Tally opening balance was not applied.
11. In `export.rs` `import_tally`, parse each file and drop its bytes before parsing the next. Today all raw files stay in memory until the import ends.
12. In `docs/guide/import-from-tally.md`, say the Day Book must start at the books beginning date. Also say to set yearly numbering before the import when Tally restarts numbers each year, and mention the Contra limit for CC and OD accounts until rank 2 is fixed.
