# India Compliance Requirements: KhataOS MVP Spec

> **Status as of:** 2026-10-19. **Scope:** Indian SMEs (proprietorships, partnerships/LLPs, private companies) keeping books in KhataOS, a local-first desktop accounting app.
> **Not legal advice.** This file turns statutes, rules and portal advisories into software requirements. Get a practising CA to confirm each item before release, and re-check GSTN and CBDT advisories every quarter. The portal-level rules change often.
>
> **Confidence tags:**
> - **[PRIMARY]** means confirmed against statute or rule text, or an official government or ICAI document.
> - **[SECONDARY]** means reported consistently by reputable professional or commentary sources, but the official text was not retrieved.
> - **[UNCERTAIN]** means sources conflict, the item is proposed but not notified, or it is interpretive. Flagged for review.

## 1. Companies Act 2013: books of account and audit trail

| Requirement | Source | Effective date | What the software must do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Books kept on **accrual basis and double-entry**, at the registered office. They may be kept in electronic mode | Companies Act s.128(1); text reproduced in the ICAI Implementation Guide on Audit Trail (2024), FAQ 1 [PRIMARY] | 1 Apr 2014 | Enforce double entry (every voucher balances). Support accrual (bills, outstanding). Proprietorships and partnerships may run cash-basis books, but the company profile must force accrual |
| Electronic books must "**remain accessible in India, at all times**" | Companies (Accounts) Rules 2014 r.3(1) [PRIMARY, via ICAI guide] | 1 Apr 2014; amended 2022 | Local-first storage on the user's machine in India meets this. Any optional cloud sync must use India-region storage. Data must never be locked up by licence expiry: guarantee read-only access forever |
| **Audit trail (edit log):** "every company which uses accounting software ... shall use only such accounting software which has a feature of recording audit trail of each and every transaction, creating an edit log of each change made in books of account along with the date when such changes were made and ensuring that the audit trail cannot be disabled" | r.3(1) proviso, inserted by GSR 205(E), 24 Mar 2021; deferred by GSR 247(E), 1 Apr 2021, then by GSR 235(E), 31 Mar 2022 [PRIMARY, ICAI guide p.3 fn.1] | **FY starting on or after 1 Apr 2023** | (a) An append-only log for every insert, update, delete or cancel on any voucher or master that affects the books (including opening balances, voucher types and ledgers). (b) Each entry records timestamp, user ID, machine/session, object ID, version number, action, and the full before/after snapshot or diff. (c) **No setting, licence tier or CLI flag can turn it off.** (d) Tamper evidence: hash-chain the log entries (each entry stores the previous entry's hash) and verify the chain on open. (e) Database-level capture: SQL triggers or a write-ahead journal, so that changes made by external tools are also logged, or at least detected. (f) A UI to view the version history of any voucher (compare the Tally Alt+Q behaviour) plus a summary report |
| What the trail should capture: **when** (date and time), **who** (user ID), **what** data changed; enabled at the database level where applicable; protected from modification; access to the trail restricted and itself logged; backups of the trail archived for the statutory period | ICAI Implementation Guide on Reporting on Audit Trail (Revised 2024), paras 20 and Glossary [PRIMARY: ICAI guidance, not statute] | Guidance for FY 2023-24 onward | Implement the full set: separate role permission for viewing the log; log every view or export of the audit trail; include the log in backups; log changes to audit-related configuration such as users and roles |
| Auditor must report whether the software had the feature, whether it **operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions**, was **not tampered with**, and was **preserved as per statutory retention** | Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules 2014 r.11(g) [PRIMARY, ICAI guide] | FY starting on or after 1 Apr 2022 (reporting) | Provide an "Audit Trail Integrity Report" for the auditor. It covers the period covered, a chain-verification result, any gaps (for example the app was offline or the log was restored from backup), users, and counts by action |
| **Retention: 8 years.** s.128(5) requires books to be preserved for at least 8 financial years, so the audit trail must be kept for at least 8 years from 1 Apr 2023 onward | Companies Act s.128(5); ICAI guide para 19 [PRIMARY] | FY 2023-24 onward | Never purge the edit log or vouchers younger than 8 FYs. "Delete company" or "split FY" must keep or export the trail. Archive format must stay readable (document the schema) |
| **Daily backup on a server physically located in India**, even where books are kept outside India | r.3(5) as amended by the Companies (Accounts) Fourth Amendment Rules 2022, notified 5 Aug 2022 (changed "periodic" to "daily") [SECONDARY: BCAJ, Grant Thornton, Cyril Amarchand] | Aug 2022 | Built-in **scheduled daily backup** to a user-chosen target (second local disk, NAS, or India-region object storage), with a backup log. **[UNCERTAIN]** whether a company's own on-premise PC in India counts as a "server physically located in India". Most practitioners read it as satisfied when primary data is on-premise in India. Default to an additional off-device daily backup and record proof |
| Annual disclosure to the ROC of the service provider's name and address, and the location of servers or backups, where books are kept electronically with a provider | r.3(6) [SECONDARY] | Aug 2022 | Report screen: "Electronic books: software, version, data location, backup location", to copy into the board report or AOC-4 |
| GST (all registered persons, **not only companies**): "where the registers and other documents are maintained electronically, **a log of every entry edited or deleted shall be maintained**"; no erasing or overwriting entries | CGST Rules r.56(8) [PRIMARY] (<https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter7/rule56_v1.00.html>) | 1 Jul 2017 | **Audit trail on for every entity type**, including proprietors and partnerships. One implementation covers both rules |
| GST electronic records: proper electronic **backup** that can be restored; produce records on demand in hard copy or a readable format; disclose file passwords and codes on demand | CGST Rules r.57 [PRIMARY] | 1 Jul 2017 | Backup and restore; export to PDF/CSV/JSON; documented open data format |
| GST retention: accounts kept for **72 months from the due date of the annual return** for that year | CGST Act s.36 [SECONDARY: standard reading, not re-fetched] | 1 Jul 2017 | Retention engine: max(8 FY Companies Act, 72 months after the GSTR-9 due date, income-tax period) |
| LLPs and non-company entities: no MCA audit-trail mandate found for LLPs | **[UNCERTAIN]**: no LLP-rule equivalent found | none | Not needed for MVP. GST r.56(8) already requires the log for LLPs |

## 2. Financial year and books rules

| Requirement | Source | Effective date | What the software must do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company FY = **1 April to 31 March** (a different period only for a holding or subsidiary of a foreign company, with Tribunal approval) | Companies Act s.2(41) [SECONDARY: well established] | 1 Apr 2014 | Default FY Apr to Mar. Allow "books beginning from" a mid-year date (new business). Do not support non-April FYs for Indian entities in the MVP |
| Income-tax: "previous year" and "assessment year" are replaced by a single **"tax year"** (the FY in which income is earned) under the **Income-tax Act 2025, in force 1 Apr 2026**. Tax-audit assist reports (turnover, cash-percentage test). **[UNCERTAIN]** exact form numbers |
| GST financial year = April to March. Invoice numbers unique per FY | CGST Rules 46(b), 53 [PRIMARY] | 1 Jul 2017 | A per-FY numbering series is mandatory. Warn on rollover |
| Period locking and year-end close | Good practice that supports s.128 and audit trail | none | A lock-date per company with an override permission. **Any change behind the lock-date is logged with a reason** |

## 4. GST

### 4.1 Identity, place of supply, tax split

| Requirement | Source | Effective date | What the software must do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GSTIN structure** (15 characters): characters 1-2 state code; 3-12 PAN; 13 entity number for the same PAN in that state (1-9 then A-Z); 14 is "Z" by default; 15 is the check character | GSTN format, widely documented [SECONDARY: TaxGuru, AI Accountant] | 1 Jul 2017 | Regex `^[0-9]{2}[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z][1-9A-Z][Z][0-9A-Z]$` (special formats exist for UIN, NRTP, TDS/TCS and OIDAR registrations, so allow an override). Check that the state code is valid and that the embedded PAN matches the party PAN where captured |
| **GSTIN checksum**: Luhn mod-36 over the first 14 characters (0-9 = 0-9, A-Z = 10-35; factors alternate 1 and 2 starting from the left; each product reduced as quotient(p/36) + remainder(p mod 36); check = (36 − sum mod 36) mod 36) | Third-party implementations, for example <https://github.com/Devil7-Softwares/GSTIN-Validator> [UNCERTAIN: GSTN has not published the algorithm in the Act or Rules] | none | Validate offline. Show a **warning, not a hard block**, if the checksum fails. Optional online "verify GSTIN status" (active, cancelled, suspended) when connected |
| State codes (GST): 01 JK, 02 HP, 03 PB, 04 CH, 05 UK, 06 HR, 07 DL, 08 RJ, 09 UP, 10 BR, 11 SK, 12 AR, 13 NL, 14 MN, 15 MZ, 16 TR, 17 ML, 18 AS, 19 WB, 20 JH, 21 OD, 22 CG, 23 MP, 24 GJ, 26 DNHDD (25 DD legacy), 27 MH, 29 KA, 30 GA, 31 LD, 32 KL, 33 TN, 34 PY, 35 AN, 36 TS, 37 AP (28 AP legacy), 38 LA, 97 Other Territory, 99 Centre Jurisdiction | GST portal state master [UNCERTAIN: compiled from product knowledge; verify against the GST portal or e-invoice master codes] | none | Ship as a versioned master table. e-Invoice and e-way bill use the same codes |
| **Place of supply (POS)**: goods per IGST s.10 (where movement terminates; bill-to-ship-to rules); services per s.12 (both parties in India: default is recipient location if registered, with special rules for immovable property, events, transport, telecom and others) and s.13 (import or export of services) | IGST Act ss.10, 12, 13 [SECONDARY: statute not re-fetched] | 1 Jul 2017 | POS engine: default from party state and ship-to state, with an override per voucher and a reason. Store POS state code on every invoice |
| **Tax type**: supplier state = POS state gives CGST + SGST/UTGST (intra-State); otherwise IGST (inter-State). Supplies to or by an **SEZ** are always inter-State (IGST). Exports are zero-rated (IGST paid or LUT) | IGST Act ss.7, 8, 16 [SECONDARY] | 1 Jul 2017 | Derive the tax components automatically and block mixing. Support LUT number and date for zero-rated supplies. Use the Rule 46 export endorsement text (see 4.2) |
| **Rates (GST 2.0)**: the four-slab structure is rationalised into **5% (merit) and 18% (standard)**, plus **40% (de-merit)** for select goods, along with the existing special rates (0, 0.25, 3 and others). New master rate notification **9/2025-Central Tax (Rate)**, 17 Sep 2025 | 56th GST Council; Notif. 9/2025-CT(R) [SECONDARY: EY alert, India Briefing] | **22 Sep 2025** (tobacco and pan masala: 1 Feb 2026) | **Rate masters must be effective-dated** (HSN to rate history), so that pre-22 Sep 2025 invoices, credit notes and amendments keep old rates. No hard-coded slab list: rates come from data |
| **Compensation cess**: removed for most goods from 22 Sep 2025. For tobacco and pan masala it ended **1 Feb 2026**, replaced by the Health Security se National Security Cess and additional central excise duty, which are outside GST | Notifications dated 31 Dec 2025 [SECONDARY: Deccan Herald, Outlook Business, Taxo] | 22 Sep 2025 / 1 Feb 2026 | Keep the cess fields in the data model, GSTR-1 and e-invoice JSON (value 0), and support historical cess. The cess rate in masters is effective-dated |

### 4.2 Invoices, numbering, notes, RCM

| Requirement | Source | Effective date | What the software must do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tax invoice mandatory particulars (Rule 46)**: (a) supplier name, address, GSTIN; (b) serial number (see next row); (c) date; (d) recipient name, address, GSTIN/UIN if registered; (e) for unregistered recipients with value of ₹50,000 or more: name, address, delivery address, **state name and code**; (f) the same below ₹50,000 if the recipient asks (for OIDAR, e-commerce or online-gaming B2C supplies: state name only); (g) **HSN/SAC**; (h) description; (i) quantity and unit/UQC; (j) total value; (k) taxable value after discount; (l) tax rates (CGST, SGST/UTGST, IGST, cess); (m) tax amounts; (n) **POS with state name** for inter-State supplies; (o) delivery address if different from POS; (p) **whether tax is payable on reverse charge**; (q) signature or digital signature (**not required for electronic invoices** under the IT Act); (r) **QR code with embedded IRN** where e-invoicing applies; (s) declaration text for e-invoice-exempt entities above the threshold. | **Invoice serial number**: "consecutive serial number **not exceeding sixteen characters**, in one or multiple series, containing alphabets or numerals or special characters **hyphen or dash and slash** ... **unique for a financial year**" | Rule 46(b) [PRIMARY]; the same wording in r.53 for revised invoices and CN/DN | 1 Jul 2017 | Series config: prefix/suffix tokens (`{FY}`, `{BR}`) with a total length of 16 or less; allowed set `[A-Za-z0-9/-]`; no spaces, `#` or `_`. Uniqueness enforced per (GSTIN, document type, FY). Gap detection report. Cancellation (Alt+X-style) keeps the number. | Copies: goods in **triplicate** (ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT, DUPLICATE FOR TRANSPORTER, TRIPLICATE FOR SUPPLIER); services in **duplicate**. Not required for e-invoices issued under r.48(4) | CGST Rule 48(1), (2), (6) [PRIMARY] | 1 Jul 2017 | Print-copy labels per copy |
| Time limit: services invoice within **30 days** of supply (45 days for banks, insurers and NBFCs); goods at or before removal | CGST Rule 47 [PRIMARY]; s.31(1) | 1 Jul 2017 | Warn when the invoice date is more than 30 days after the linked delivery or service date |
| Other documents: Bill of Supply (exempt or composition), Receipt Voucher (advance), Refund Voucher, Payment Voucher (RCM), Delivery Challan | CGST Rules 49 to 52, 55 [SECONDARY] | 1 Jul 2017 | Voucher types with their own number series |
| **Credit and debit notes (s.34; Rule 53(1A))**: fields are supplier details, nature of document, serial number (same 16-character rule), date, recipient details, **original invoice number(s) and date(s)**, taxable value, rate and tax credited or debited, signature. **CN time limit**: by **30 Nov** following the end of the FY of the supply, or the date of the annual return, whichever is earlier | CGST Rule 53 [PRIMARY]; s.34(2) as amended by FA 2022 s.102 [SECONDARY] | Rule 53(1A) from 1 Feb 2019; 30 Nov from 1 Oct 2022 (Notif. 18/2022-CT) | Link each CN/DN to its original invoice(s). Block or warn on a CN dated after 30 Nov of FY+1 that reduces output tax. A separate number series for CN and DN |
| Revised invoice, and "INPUT TAX CREDIT NOT ADMISSIBLE" on documents issued under ss.74, 129 or 130 | Rule 53(1), (3) [PRIMARY] | 1 Jul 2017 | Voucher sub-type flags |
| **Reverse charge (RCM)**: notified goods and services (s.9(3)); specified supplies from unregistered persons (s.9(4)). The recipient issues a **self-invoice** (s.31(3)(f)) **within 30 days** of receipt from an unregistered supplier (**Rule 47A**), and a payment voucher at the time of payment (s.31(3)(g)). The monthly consolidated self-invoice proviso was **omitted** from 1 Nov 2024. RCM tax is paid in **cash**; ITC follows | CGST Rule 47A and Notif. 20/2024-CT [SECONDARY]; omission of the r.46 proviso w.e.f. 1 Nov 2024 [PRIMARY: r.46 footnote 11] | 1 Nov 2024 | RCM flag per ledger or service. Auto-draft the self-invoice with a 30-day reminder. Liability goes to GSTR-3B 3.1(d). ITC appears only after cash payment. "Reverse charge: Yes" on the invoice |
| e-Invoicing for RCM self-invoices (new supply types RCMDOM and RCMIMP) | GSTN Advisory reported as No. 678, 16 Oct 2026; **sandbox only** [UNCERTAIN: secondary reports only, production date not announced] | not yet | Design the e-invoice module so that new supply types are data-driven. Watch for the production date |

### 4.5 e-Invoicing (IRP)

| Requirement | Source | Effective date | What the software must do |
|---|---|---|---|
